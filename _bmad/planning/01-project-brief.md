# Project Brief

Build a demonstrable Workforce Intelligence application on Cloudera that makes the platform capabilities visible through a real business scenario rather than a product-only demo. The solution must combine structured candidate data, unstructured CV/policy content, governed analytics and AI-driven experiences.

The PoC should feel like one business product while the technical walkthrough can expose Cloudera DataFlow, CDE, CDW, Cloudera AI, SDX and Data Visualization underneath.
